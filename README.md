# YouTube TV NoAds

Experimental ad-filter patcher for the original YouTube Android TV app. Keeps the original interface and builds one APK with its own TV tile.

- **Tested TV:** Sony Bravia X90H, Android TV 12
- **YouTube version:** 5.30.320, ARMv7 (`armeabi-v7a`)
- **Status:** Experimental. Complete ad blocking and compatibility with other TVs are not guaranteed.

Bring your own matching original APKs. No YouTube APKs are distributed here.
