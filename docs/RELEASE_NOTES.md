First experimental source-only release of **YouTube TV NoAds**.

- Builds one locally signed APK with a **YouTube TV NoAds** Android TV tile.
- Original YouTube Android TV **5.30.320 / ARMv7** profile only; mismatched APKs are rejected.
- Tested on **Sony Bravia X90H / Android TV 12**.
- Includes our patcher, local response filter, native hook, build instructions and source checks.

Bring your own matching original APKs and build locally. **No YouTube APK, signing key, device logs or account information is included.**

Ad filtering and device compatibility remain experimental. One pause of unknown cause was observed during the mixed playback/functionality session. This release does not claim complete ad suppression or long-term stability. See [test results](https://github.com/pearlxcore/YouTube-TV-NoAds/blob/v0.1.0/docs/TESTING.md) and [build instructions](https://github.com/pearlxcore/YouTube-TV-NoAds/blob/v0.1.0/docs/BUILD.md).
